# Sol de Janeiro discovery set: what's inside each one, what it costs, and how to pay less for it

The appeal of a Sol de Janeiro discovery set is simple: the brand's full-size body mists and creams sit in treat-yourself territory, and 30 ml bottles of the same juice cost a fraction of that. The annoying part is figuring out which set is actually on sale where, because Sol de Janeiro rotates them constantly, and most of them are labelled limited edition.

So here's the practical version — what's in the current sets, what each one costs per bottle, why half of them show as out of stock, and how to claw a bit of the money back when you buy. Prices and stock move fast, so treat every number below as "what the listing said when checked" rather than gospel.

## What's actually inside these sets

Almost everything sold under the "discovery set" label is travel sizing. Perfume mists come as 30 ml (1 oz) bottles, body creams as 1.7 oz jars. Sets run from two pieces to five, and the scents included change with each release.

If you've never bought the brand before, the scent numbers mean nothing on their own, so here's the cheat sheet. Cheirosa is Portuguese for smelling delicious, and each number is a different fragrance:

- **62** — pistachio, salted caramel, vanilla. The one that made the brand famous.
- **59** — vanilla orchid, sugared violet, sheer sandalwood. Warmer and woodier.
- **68** — Brazilian jasmine, pink dragonfruit, vanilla. Fruity floral.
- **40** — black amber plum, jasmine blooms, vanilla woods.
- **91** — passionfruit, rose, honey caramel. The newest of the main line.
- **87** — solar tuberose, coconut milk, warm sand.
- **76** — blackcurrant, midnight jasmine, amber woods.

Body care sets swap the mists for the creams: Brazilian Bum Bum Cream, Bom Dia Bright, Delícia Drench. Those are 1.7 oz travel jars too, so a "three cream" set is roughly a week or two of daily use if you're generous with it.

## The price-per-bottle maths

This is where the sets stop being interchangeable.

The Ulta three-piece at $19.60 works out to about **$6.53 per 1 oz bottle**. The Sephora four-piece at $36 is **$9 per bottle**. The UK five-piece at £40 is **£8 each**. The Lookfantastic four-piece at £35 comes to £8.75 a mist, though that one throws in a 10 ml lotion, which nudges the value back up. On the Irish listing, the 25% discount takes it to €30.19, or roughly €7.55 per mist.

The Ultá body cream trio is the one that looks strangest on paper: $26.60 for a set the retailer values at $54, which is about **$8.87 per 1.7 oz jar**. If you've been meaning to try Delícia Drench or Bom Dia Bright without committing to a 240 ml tub, that's the cheapest route in.

The honest caveat: a 30 ml mist is small. How long it lasts depends entirely on how you spray. Someone who does two spritzes on the wrists will get months out of a bottle. Someone who mists hair, clothes and linens daily will finish a set in a few weeks. That's not a knock on the product — it's just the reason the per-bottle maths only tells you so much.

## Why the sets keep disappearing

Three of the listings above were flagged as out of stock or pre-order when checked: the Sephora US four-piece was marked out of stock and limited edition, the Sephora UK four-piece was "temporarily out of stock", and Space NK's Australian five-piece was listed as pre-order.

That's normal for this category rather than a sign of anything dramatic. Discovery sets are usually seasonal or event-driven — gift set season, a new scent launch, a retailer's exclusive — and once the production run sells through, it doesn't come back. Some are outright retailer exclusives, like the Urban Outfitters double-date duo, which isn't stocked anywhere else.

The practical takeaway: if you see the set you want at a price you're comfortable with, don't spend three weeks deliberating. Restock notices are unreliable, and the next release usually swaps at least one scent.

## Which set fits which person

**New to the brand and don't know what you like:** the five-piece at £40, or the Sephora four-piece at $36 if you're in the US. Both give you four or five different scents in one go, which is the entire point of a discovery set.

**Already know Cheirosa 62 is your thing:** the Ulta three-piece at $19.60 is the cheapest per bottle in this lineup, and you get 40 and 59 alongside it. If you hate those two, you've still paid under $20.

**Here for the body care, not the mists:** the Ulta cream trio at $26.60 is the entry point that makes sense, because 1.7 oz of Bum Bum Cream, Bom Dia Bright and Delícia Drench tells you whether you want to spend on a full tub.

**Want a travel kit that covers scent and skin:** the Lookfantastic four-mist set with the 10 ml Body Badalada Lotion, or Space NK's Find Your Sol set, which bundles the same lotion with four mists including 91 and 68.

**Buying as a gift:** the sets with mixed contents photograph better and suit a wider range of tastes — a mist-and-cream duo beats four bottles of something the recipient might not wear.

**Already converted and buying full sizes anyway:** the official $150 wardrobe set, if the pick-your-own format appeals more than another round of travel sizes.

## Quick answers

**Are the mists full size?** No. The mists in these sets are 30 ml / 1 oz, and the creams are 1.7 oz. Full-size mists are 90 ml.

**Is the set cheaper than buying the same scents separately?** Per millilitre, yes — every listing above is priced below the stated value of its contents. Whether that matters depends on whether you'll use all of the scents, not on the percentage saved.

**Why is the same set a different price in different countries?** Because they're different retailer listings in different currencies, with different local taxes and promotions. Regional pricing on this brand varies a lot.

**Can I earn cashback if I buy in the retailer's app?** For Sephora, no — purchases through the Sephora app are excluded. Use the website after clicking through from the cashback platform.

**Do the sets come back in stock?** Sometimes, briefly. Most are limited editions tied to a season or a launch, and the next version usually includes different scents.

**Do I need to buy anything else to make the cashback worth it?** No, but the numbers make more sense on a larger order, or when combined with a sign-up bonus. On a single $19.60 set, cashback is a rounding error.

The short version: pick the set whose contents you'll actually use, check whether it's in stock in your own market, and route the purchase through a cashback link so the same order costs you slightly less. Small win, no downside.
